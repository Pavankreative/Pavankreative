 🧭  About Me
<img align="right" width="300" src="https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/74038190/212257472-08e52665-c503-4bd9-aa20-f5a4dae769b5.gif">

I'm a Mechanical Engineer and Independent Researcher based in India, working at the intersection of engineering design, simulation, and computational analysis. My focus is on turning hands-on hardware prototypes — electric vehicle systems, embedded control architectures, and CFD studies — into rigorously documented, peer-reviewed research.

🔬 Currently developing research manuscripts on electric two-wheeler retrofit systems, spanning propulsion authorization control and reusable systems-engineering methodology
🌀 Background in CFD & FEA analysis using ANSYS Workbench, with ongoing airfoil aerodynamics studies
🛠️ Full engineering pipeline experience: CAD design → fabrication → simulation → technical documentation → publication
🤖 Building AI/ML-assisted engineering tools, including a CFD result analysis application
🎓 Actively pursuing Project Assistant / Research Associate / JRF roles at research institutes, with a longer-term goal of a Master's degree abroad
